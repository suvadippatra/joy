How to Use

1. Select Files: Click the dashed box to select two or more PDF files from your computer.

2. Order Matters: The files will be stitched together in the exact order they appear in the list.

3. Merge: Click the Merge button. This happens entirely in your browser—no data is sent to a server.

4. Preview & Download: Once finished, you can preview the merged document below, open it in your native browser viewer (recommended for Android), or download it directly.
