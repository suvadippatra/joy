How to Use

1. The Client-Side Reality: Directly editing vector objects inside a PDF content stream via a web browser often corrupts the file. This tool instead uses "Precision Redaction Masking".

2. Configure the Mask: Move the sliders to position the red-bordered masking box over the area where the watermark exists on the simulation page.

3. Mask Color: Ensure the mask color matches your document background (usually White).

4. Erase: Click the Erase button. The tool will permanently bake your solid color mask over that exact coordinate on every page of the document.
