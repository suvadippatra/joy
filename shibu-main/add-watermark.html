How to Use

1. Live Simulation: The box on the right represents the exact shape of your uploaded PDF. As you move the sliders, you will see a buttery-smooth preview of exactly how your watermark will sit.

2. Apply: Click "Apply & Download". The app will use your simulated math (converting center-rotations to baseline-rotations) to permanently bake the watermark into every page of the real PDF.

Note on Default Logo: The default SVG logo requires a password to unlock.
