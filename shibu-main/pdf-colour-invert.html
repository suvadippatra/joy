How to Use

1. Select File: Upload a highly bright or stark-white PDF document.

2. Generate: Click the button to load the document into the preview frame.

3. Dark Mode CSS: The app applies a mathematical hue-rotation and inversion to the frame. The white paper becomes dark gray, black text becomes white, but colored images try to remain visually accurate.

Use Case: Perfect for reading long, blinding documents at night without straining your eyes.
